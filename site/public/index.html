Reachable anywhere, invisible to everyone else

  • Anyone on your guest list can open your pen's sites from anywhere in the world — home, a coffee shop, or across the planet.
  • To everyone else, your pen isn't even on the map: no public address, and not even a login page for bots, creepers, or AI scrapers to bang on.

Your normal browser, zero drama

  • Open Chrome, Safari, or Firefox like usual — your regular internet stays untouched, and nobody is routing your browsing through a VPN.
  • One tiny helper handles the pen's private addresses in the background, so every site inside loads with a real HTTPS padlock.

Locked between your devices

  • Everything inside your pen is encrypted straight from one device to the other.
  • Hogman runs the front gate and hands out the map — even we can't read your chats, photos, or conspiracy theories.

Tight leash, fast boot

  • Joining a pen never opens your laptop to anyone — personal devices take zero visitors, only the web servers you deliberately add.
  • If someone goes weird and starts pitching crypto, one click kicks every device they own off the property in about two seconds.

One request on the wire

# gary opens https://deadmans-switch.john-mcafee.pens.hogman.network

1 PAC     127.0.0.1:<port> catches *.john-mcafee.pens.hogman.network;
          all non-pen traffic bypasses Hogman completely
2 DNS     pen registrar (mesh :53) -> 100.80.12.7 (random CGNAT)
          verified peer via tsnet WhoIs (never LAN/127.0.0.1)
3 policy  Headscale ACL: gary -> tag:server :443 ONLY
4 tunnel  WireGuard E2E encrypted (direct UDP or DERP ciphertext)
          hogman-join forwards blind TCP CONNECT bytes
5 TLS 1.3 leaf cert served by the share (CertMagic local key)
          verified by Gary's stock OS trust store
✓ page loads, zero public IP, relay/host see only ciphertext

# stranger or scanner resolves the same hostname
✗ DNS     ns1 returns NODATA (zero A/AAAA records exist publicly)

Unprivileged client (hogman-join)

  • Embeds Tailscale's userspace tsnet as your user — no TUN interface, no sudo, no /etc/hosts edits, and zero conflict with system Tailscale.
  • A PAC-scoped loopback proxy hands pen hostnames into a blind TCP CONNECT tunnel and never terminates TLS.
  • Pins every target IP against tsnet WhoIs (prevents DNS rebinding to 127.0.0.1 or your LAN) and rejects every port except :443.

Split-horizon DNS-01 (real TLS, zero public IPs)

  • Shares run embedded CertMagic locally — private TLS keys never leave the server, and browsers verify certs with your stock OS trust store.
  • Validates Let's Encrypt certs (Google Trust Services fallback) via ns1.hogman.network, which serves only _acme-challenge.* TXT, SOA, NS, and CAA — zero public A/AAAA records exist.
  • Catch: hostnames appear in public CT logs, and until pens.hogman.network joins the Public Suffix List, use a separate browser profile for pens you don't fully trust.

Default-deny ACLs & 2 s revocation

  • Personal devices accept zero inbound connections. Headscale ACLs grant enrolled nodes :443 toward tag:server shares only — no SSH, no UDP, no lateral client traffic, no exit nodes.
  • Static folder shares confine reads inside Go's os.Root (symlink escape refused), and serverMayUse locks shares to their own names.
  • Kicking a member drops their devices, keys, and ACLs in ~2 s and permanently retires their handle and site names.

Host isolation & verifiable builds

  • Every pen runs in its own isolated container bridge with non-root (uid 65532), read-only containers (cap_drop: [ALL]), and Unix-socket registrar isolation.
  • Release binaries are built exclusively in GitHub Actions with SLSA provenance — verify before running:
gh attestation verify hogman-join --repo celtiberi/hogman

What it's built from

  • Headscale + Tailscale tsnet (WireGuard mesh, random CGNAT IPs)
  • CertMagic + Caddy + authoritative miekg/dns DNS-01 server
  • Go registrar (ACLs, DNS, Unix-socket API), hogman-join, sign-up broker

Why not just use Tailscale?

If you only want your own devices connected, do — Hogman is built on Tailscale's open-source tsnet and runs cleanly alongside it. Hogman is for running a private web for a group of people: guests join from a single-use link, you manage the herd from a web page, and every site gets real names and standard HTTPS with zero public IPs. The honest bit

We run the pen's control plane. Everything is AGPL-3.0 so you can check our work — and here is exactly what trusting us means.

Private, not anonymous

The keeper — and we, because we host the control plane — can see who is in the pen, their devices, and which pen names get looked up. Payloads stay encrypted end to end over WireGuard and TLS 1.3, and your normal browsing never touches the pen.

Admission & CT logs

Because our gate and ns1 control admission and DNS-01 challenges, a compromised host could try to slip in a device or mint a cert — and every cert appears in public CT logs, so it can't happen silently (threat model).

No backdoors, no recovery

We never let anyone back into a pen on request, not even its keeper. Lose every device and you start over — so make a friend a second owner. Joining a pen means trusting its Top Hog: pick a good one, or be one.
