Managing access & offboarding

For pen owners and admins. Manage staff, outside contractors, invites, and active bridges from your pen's management portal at https://home.<yourpen>.pens.hogman.network.

Roles and permissions

RolePermissions
OwnerFull administrative control. Cannot be suspended or removed. Every operational pen should have at least two owners: if one owner loses every enrolled device, only another owner can invite them back. Owners can demote one another, so grant Owner only to fully trusted principals.
AdminCreate and revoke invites; suspend, resume, and remove regular members. Cannot modify owners or other admins.
MemberEnroll personal workstations and register HTTPS bridges or internal apps. Creating invites is restricted to owners and admins unless explicitly enabled for members.

Inviting staff and contractors

Under Invite someone, add a short tracking note (for example, "Jordan — refrigeration contractor, Store North") and press Make an invite. The portal generates a single-use link and QR code valid for 7 days. Treat every invite link like a physical key — whoever opens it first enrolls.

Unused links appear under Open invites and can be revoked at any time before redemption.

Suspending and offboarding (~2 seconds)

The Members directory lists every enrolled person with their role, status, and device count (paginated with search):

  • Suspend (~2 s): immediately cuts off all of a person's devices while keeping their account, device slots, and site names intact. Ideal for outside service vendors between work orders or temporary leave.
  • Resume (<1 s): restores access immediately when a contractor or employee needs access again.
  • Remove (~2 s): ends membership, deletes every device from the mesh, revokes unused keys and invites, rewrites ACLs, and permanently retires their handle and site names so nobody can impersonate them later. Re-entry requires a new invite.
  • Promote / demote: grant or revoke Admin access on a member, or promote an Admin to Owner (owner-only).

Zero account recovery — appoint a second owner: there is no support backdoor or password reset. If someone loses every enrolled device, issue a new invite, restore their role, and remove the old account (demote an old owner to Admin first). Always keep at least two owners on every production pen.

Devices and active services

Each member manages their own devices from the portal: Add a device creates a 1-hour personal join link for a second laptop, and Get a share link creates a 1-hour server link for an on-site bridge or always-on host (Bridging equipment & apps). Active dashboards and bridged controllers appear under Sites in this pen with their owner and optional note.

Audit visibility and privacy boundaries

Owners and admins can see who belongs to the pen, their enrolled devices, online status, and which pen hostnames are queried. Neither pen owners nor Hogman's control plane can passively read application traffic (WireGuard + TLS 1.3 end to end) or any browsing outside the pen. Remind staff that bridged hostnames appear in public Certificate Transparency logs (names only, never contents) and to use a separate browser profile for any pen they do not fully trust.
