Quickstart

Take equipment controllers and internal web tools off the open internet in four steps — reachable by authorized staff in ordinary browsers over standard HTTPS, with zero public IP addresses.

1. Plan pens by site or region

Before creating your first pen, decide how to group locations. For multi-site businesses, create a separate pen per region or store group (for example, region-north and region-south, or store-north) rather than putting every location in one shared network. Separate pens limit blast radius if a laptop is lost or an account is compromised.

2. Create a pen

Request access or create your pen at {{WORK_SIGNUP_LABEL}} and claim a neutral name for your network (for example, store-north). Hogman spins up an isolated private DNS zone (store-north.pens.hogman.network) and a web management portal where owners and admins control access.

When setup finishes, the sign-up page gives you your first single-use join link.

3. Join a workstation and bridge an internal service

Open your join link on a Mac or Linux computer and run the short command it displays in Terminal:

$ curl -fsSL https://store-north.gate.hogman.network/start | sh

The script prompts for your invite link, verifies hogman-join against its published SHA-256 checksum, saves it to ~/hogman-join, and connects your browser to the pen. Ordinary internet browsing stays untouched.

Next, close any public router port-forwarding pointing at your internal device or web app, then bridge its local web interface into the pen:

$ ~/hogman-join share cooler-1 http://192.168.1.40 --background

✓ https://cooler-1.store-north.pens.hogman.network is up.
  Everyone in the pen can open it.

The local upstream can speak plain HTTP on your LAN while staff always connect over standard HTTPS (port 443) with a publicly trusted Let's Encrypt certificate and zero open firewall ports. For headless Raspberry Pi or mini PC site bridges, see Bridging equipment & apps.

4. Onboard staff and appoint a second owner

Open your pen's management portal (https://home.<yourpen>.pens.hogman.network) to create single-use invite links for employees and outside contractors, suspend temporary access, or offboard someone from every device in ~2 seconds.

Appoint a backup owner right away: Hogman has zero account recovery and no support backdoor. If a pen's only owner loses every enrolled device, nobody can administer that pen again. Promote a second trusted owner under Members as soon as they join.
