Running your pen

For the keeper (the pen's owner) and anyone they make an admin. Everything here is on your pen's home page, https://home.yourpen.pens.hogman.network.

Roles

RoleCan
Owner (the keeper)Everything. Can't be suspended or removed. A pen can have more than one owner, and should: an owner who loses every device needs another owner to invite them back. Owners can demote each other, so make owners only of people you trust completely.
AdminMake and revoke invites, suspend, resume and remove members. Can't act on the owner or other admins.
MemberJoin devices, claim names, run servers and shares. Inviting is keepers-only unless the pen allows members to invite.

Inviting people

Under Invite someone, add a note for yourself ("Alice, from the climbing club") and press Make an invite. You get a link and a QR code. Each invite works for one person, once, and expires after 7 days. Send it however you like: the link is the only way in, so treat it like a key.

Open invites lists the ones nobody has used yet; revoke any of them. (Invite links from before public certificates — carrying fp= — no longer work; old pens created on .hogman zones must be recreated.)

The herd

The herd lists everyone in the pen with their role, status and number of devices, 20 at a time, with a search box, so it stays quick in a pen of thousands. For each member:

  • Suspend: all their devices are cut off within seconds. Their membership, devices and names are kept. Use it when you're not sure yet.
  • Resume: back in, in under a second.
  • Kick (the boot): the membership ends, every device is deleted, unused keys and invites they made are revoked, and their site names are retired permanently so nobody can pretend to be them. Coming back takes a new invite.
  • Make admin or back to member, and Make owner for an admin (owner only).
  • Someone lost every device? There is no account recovery. Invite them again, give the new account the old one's role, and kick the old account (make an old owner an admin first).

Devices and names

Every member manages their own devices on the home page: Add a device for a one-time join link, and Get a share link to put something in the pen from a computer that isn't in it. Names belong to shares, which name themselves. Every site hostname gets a standard web certificate, so its hostname appears in public Certificate Transparency logs (contents stay private).

What to tell your members

As keeper you can see who is in the pen, their devices, when they're online and which pen names they look up. You can't see what members send each other, or anything they do outside the pen. Let members know that pen and shared-site names are public in CT logs, and that if they join other hosted pens under pens.hogman.network, they should use a separate browser profile for pens they don't fully trust (D-19).
