Running a pen

For keepers (owners) and admins. Everything here lives on your pen's home page at https://home.<yourpen>.pens.hogman.network.

Roles

RolePermissions
Owner (keeper)Full control. Cannot be suspended or kicked. A pen can — and should — have more than one owner: if an owner loses every device, only another owner can invite them back. Owners can demote each other, so appoint only people you trust completely.
AdminCreate and revoke invites; suspend, resume, and kick regular members. Cannot act on owners or other admins.
MemberEnroll personal devices and host HTTPS servers. Creating invites is keepers-only unless enabled for members.

Inviting people

Under Invite someone, add a short note for yourself (e.g. "Gary, from the climbing club") and press Make an invite. You get a single-use link and QR code valid for 7 days. Treat the link like a key — anyone who opens it first gets in.

Open invites lists unused links so you can revoke any before they're redeemed.

The herd: suspend & kick

The herd lists every member with their role, status, and device count (paginated with search):

  • Suspend (~2 s): cuts off all of a member's devices while keeping their account, devices, and site names intact. Use when you want a reversible timeout.
  • Resume (<1 s): restores access immediately.
  • Kick / the boot (~2 s): ends membership, deletes every device from the mesh, revokes their unused keys and invites, rewrites ACLs, and permanently retires their handle and site names so nobody can impersonate them. Coming back requires a fresh invite.
  • Promote / demote: make a member an admin (or back to member), or promote an admin to owner (owner-only).

No account recovery: if a member loses every device, invite them again, restore their role, and kick the old account (demote an old owner to admin first). Appoint a second owner so your pen can never be locked out.

Devices & site names

Every member manages their own devices from the home page: Add a device creates a 1-hour personal join link, and Get a share link creates a 1-hour server link for hosting from an always-on machine (Hosting HTTPS servers). Active sites appear under Sites in this pen with their owner and optional note.

What keepers can (and can't) see

As keeper, you can see who is in the pen, their devices, online status, and which pen names are queried. You cannot passively read what members send each other (WireGuard + TLS 1.3 end to end) or any browsing outside the pen. Let members know that site hostnames appear in public Certificate Transparency logs and to use a separate browser profile for pens they don't fully trust. See the full threat model.
