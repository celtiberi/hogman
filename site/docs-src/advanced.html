Advanced less safe

Sometimes the best security fix for a surface is just to delete the surface.

That's how Hogman is built. A pen carries HTTPS to its own names, and nothing else: no ssh, no other ports, no games, no raw network access between devices. Every one of those would be one more way in for an attacker, on your computer and on everyone else's.

This page is for people who know exactly what they're giving up and want it anyway. Nothing here is needed to use a pen. If you're not sure, don't.

Joining through your own Tailscale app

Normally a computer joins through Hogman's own small connection, which runs as you (never as administrator) and carries HTTPS to the pen's names only. You can instead join through the Tailscale app on your computer:

$ ./hogman-join --tailscale '<your link>'

What that opens up:

  • Your whole computer is on the pen, at the network level. Every program on it, including anything malicious, and every other account on the computer, can reach the pen as you.
  • Your Tailscale app can only be on one network at a time. If it's on another one (your work network, say), joining the pen switches it off that one until you switch back (add --switch to allow it).
  • Your computer takes its DNS settings from the pen's control server. A trustworthy pen only answers its own names, but a hostile keeper could point those names at your own computer or your router, or tell your computer to send all its DNS lookups to them, so they could see and redirect your ordinary browsing. (The usual connection never does this: it answers only the pen's names and only ever connects to the pen's own devices.)
  • What stays the same: other members still can't connect to your computer (personal devices accept nothing), and the pen's rules still only let you reach its servers on ports 443 and 80. The Tailscale app is told to refuse any routes the pen offers.

Only do this for a pen whose keeper you'd trust with your computer's DNS. To go back to the usual way: ./hogman-join leave '<your link>', then join again without --tailscale.

Running your own web server

Still HTTPS only, but you run and patch the web server yourself instead of letting hogman-join share do it: Your own web server. Everything you run on it is reachable by every member of the pen, so keep it updated and give it nothing it doesn't need.

What Hogman deliberately doesn't do

  • Other ports and protocols (ssh, databases, game servers, voice over UDP): the pen's rules allow only 443 and 80 to servers, and its connection only carries those. If you need them, a tool built for that (your own Tailscale network, say) is the right one.
  • Exit nodes and subnet routes (sending your internet, or your home network, through the pen): off, and not planned.
  • Reaching other members' computers: personal devices accept nothing, ever.

We may add more, slowly and deliberately, once the core is solid and each addition has been attacked on purpose first. Until then, the safest feature is the one that isn't there.
