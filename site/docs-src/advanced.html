Advanced less safe

Sometimes the best security fix for a surface is just to delete the surface.

That's how Hogman is built. A pen carries HTTPS to its own names, and nothing else: no ssh, no other ports, no games, no raw network access between devices. Every one of those would be one more way in for an attacker, on your computer and on everyone else's.

This page is for people who want to know where the line is, and what's on the other side of it. Nothing here is needed to use a pen. If you're not sure, don't.

Running your own web server

Still HTTPS only, but you run and patch the web server yourself instead of letting hogman-join share do it: Your own web server. Everything you run on it is reachable by every member of the pen, so keep it updated and give it nothing it doesn't need.

What Hogman deliberately doesn't do

  • Joining through your own Tailscale app. It used to be an option. It put every program on the member's computer (malware included) onto the pen at the network level, and handed the pen's control server that computer's DNS. That's one member widening the risk for everyone in the pen, without the keeper getting a say, so it's gone. Every computer joins through Hogman's own small connection.
  • Other ports and protocols (ssh, databases, game servers, voice over UDP): the pen's rules allow only 443 and 80 to servers, and its connection only carries those. If you need them, a tool built for that (your own Tailscale network, say) is the right one.
  • Exit nodes and subnet routes (sending your internet, or your home network, through the pen): off, and not planned.
  • Reaching other members' computers: personal devices accept nothing, ever.

We may add more, slowly and deliberately, once the core is solid and each addition has been attacked on purpose first. Until then, the safest feature is the one that isn't there.
