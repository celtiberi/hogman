Hogman docs

Most people need three things: get in, put something in, and run the pen. Start there. The technical pages are further down, for when you want to know more.

1. Joining a pen

Open your invite link, copy one command, and you're in. Then add your other computers.

2. Putting things in your pen

One command puts a folder, an app or a website in the pen with its own name and the padlock: hogman-join share photos ~/holiday.
