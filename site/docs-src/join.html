Joining a pen

You need an invite link from someone in the pen and a Mac or Linux computer. It takes about a minute.

Your first computer · Two ways in · Side by side, in practice · More devices · Leaving · Options

Your first computer

  1. Open your invite link on the computer you want to join. Invites work once and expire after 7 days.
  2. Copy the command it shows into a terminal. It downloads hogman-join for your computer and runs it with your link: 
    $ curl -fsSLo hogman-join https://smith.pens.hogman.network/join/dl/hogman-join-Darwin-arm64 \
    && chmod +x hogman-join \
    && ./hogman-join 'https://smith.pens.hogman.network/join#t=…&fp=…'
  3. Pick your name when asked: lowercase letters, digits and dashes, 2 to 32 characters. It's how the pen knows you.
  4. Enter your password once, so the pen's certificate can be added to your computer. On a Mac this has to happen at the Mac itself, not over SSH: macOS only lets a person approve a new trusted certificate on screen.
  5. You're in. It checks your pen's home page loads over HTTPS and opens it. Keep the hogman-join file: you'll use it to share things and to leave.

Before trusting anything, the tool checks the pen's certificate against the fingerprint in your link, and that the certificate can only vouch for the pen's own names. A link that doesn't match is refused before the invite is used.

Two ways in

The tool picks one by itself and tells you which:

  • With your computer's Tailscale, if it's installed and not already on another network. The pen becomes a separate Tailscale profile, so any other networks are kept (tailscale switch --list). Every app on the computer can reach the pen.
  • Side by side, if Tailscale isn't installed or is busy on another network (your work tailnet, say). A small connection of its own runs in the background, starts by itself when you log in, and offers a local proxy that carries only the pen's names. Your other network and everything else on the computer are left alone. Force it with --alongside, or switch your Tailscale over instead with --switch.

Side by side, in practice

Only programs that use the proxy reach the pen. The join tool prints your proxy address (something like http://127.0.0.1:21234) and sets things up:

  • Browsers: on a Mac, the tool sets the automatic proxy setting for you (it never replaces one someone else set). On Linux and Windows, set your browser's or desktop's automatic proxy configuration URL to the proxy.pac address it prints. The rules file sends only pen names through the pen.
  • curl and most command-line tools, per command: 
    $ https_proxy=http://127.0.0.1:21234 curl https://blog.smith.hogman
    Don't export it for the whole shell: the proxy refuses everything outside the pen.
  • git, per site: 
    $ git config --global http.https://git.smith.hogman/.proxy http://127.0.0.1:21234
  • ssh: 
    $ ssh -o ProxyCommand='nc -X connect -x 127.0.0.1:21234 %h %p' box.smith.hogman

More devices

On your pen's home page, press Add a device. You get a one-time link (and a QR code) that works once and expires in an hour. Open it on the other computer and copy the command, as before; the device joins as you. Phones can't join yet; the app is in the works.

For a machine that should host things, press Add a server instead, or skip that and share from a computer that's already in.

Leaving

$ ./hogman-join leave '<your link>'

This takes down anything you share from this computer, removes the pen's certificate, stops the side-by-side connection or logs Tailscale out of the pen, and removes its saved profile. Your membership stays; the keeper removes members.

Options

--handle NAMEYour name in the pen, instead of being asked.
--hostname NAMEWhat this device is called (default: the computer's name).
--alongsideJoin side by side even if Tailscale is free.
--switchUse this computer's Tailscale even if it's on another network (that one pauses).
--no-serviceSide by side: run in the background without starting at login.
--no-system-proxySide by side: don't change the Mac's proxy setting.
--dry-runCheck the link and the pen, change nothing.
--no-trustDon't add the pen's certificate (you'll handle trust yourself).
--no-openDon't open the home page at the end.

Run ./hogman-join --help for the full list.
