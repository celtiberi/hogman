        The herd

        Everyone in the pen. Suspending cuts all their devices off at once and can be undone. Kicking ends their membership for good and permanently retires their names.

        You're the only owner. If you lose every device, nobody can manage this pen again — there is no account recovery. Make someone you trust completely an owner (make them an admin, then an owner).

        Someone lost every device? There is no account recovery: invite them again as a new member, give them back their role, then kick the old account (an old owner account has to be made an admin first).
